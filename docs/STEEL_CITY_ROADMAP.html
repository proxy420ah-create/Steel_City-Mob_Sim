Current Work — Autonomous Combat Perception (bridge into Gang Sim)

Just landed (Oct 29): gunshot-report hearing + the aim rig, on top of the authored reload states. Every real shot now broadcasts a semantic report through SfxWaves.Report → static Heard event — receivers self-filter (not self, inside AmplitudeAt earshot ~50m, shooter is their threat/watch target) → OnShotHeard snaps belief to the firing position and re-anchors a live search orbit. Intel, not a sighting: contactSeen/LastSeenAt stay on the eye channel, so a heard shot can never make someone shootable. Possess-fired shots inform AI too — the player is no longer silent.
Aim rig (phases 1–2, playtest-validated): one focal point drives the solve — arm two-bone IK, torso stance blade (waist yaw swinging the gun shoulder forward — v1's roll-about-`d̂` tilt was reverted), head-quat eyes-on-threat tracking through cover legs, and the stride-7 IK buffer carrying a dormant second arm pair for the two-handed rifle work.
Reload (authored, not solved): states 13/14 in character_animator.html — the support arm reaches and taps once per RoundSeated seat (the same tick as reload_click), roles mirrored by reloadGunSide so the free hand always does the work.
Next: widen the hearing scope — citizens/bystanders react to a stranger's bang (turn-to-sound, flee, report → awareness table per PERCEPTION_AWARENESS.md); health/wound layer (Flinch→Falling→Down states exist, no attrition yet); aim-rig phase 3 (bore-on-line arm solve). Then resume Phase 4d: HoodAgent + order queue. Perf gate stands: hold ~120 fps before further city expansion.
